Puerto Vallarta — Dad's 80th Birthday

April 10–17, 2027 · 7 nights, arrive on the birthday

6 adults in 4 rooms. All-inclusive resorts, pickleball on site is a must, shuffleboard a plus, and somewhere good for daily walks.

Nuevo Vallarta / Bucerías

Walking here is the beach itself — long, flat sand. No town malecón.

Marival Emotions Resort & Suites, Nuevo Vallarta — $$

3 pickleball courts with equipment included, shuffleboard, 6 pools, 7 restaurants, beachfront, about 20 minutes from the airport. Big lively family resort.

Marival Emotions — what's included

Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit, Nuevo Vallarta — $$$$$

4 pickleball courts, best food of the bunch, all suites with big terraces, beachfront, about 20 minutes from the airport. Upscale and quiet. No shuffleboard.

Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit

Paradise Village Beach Resort and Spa, Nuevo Vallarta — $

Pickleball at the on-site El Tigre racquet club, shuffleboard, 900 ft of beach, next to the marina and shops so the best walking of the Nuevo resorts. All-suite, condo-style. (Room-only rate shown; all-inclusive is an add-on.)

Paradise Village

Marival Distinct All-Suites & World Spa, Nuevo Vallarta — $$$

1 pickleball court (reservation only), spa-focused, quieter and more grown-up feel than its sister resort next door. Shuffleboard is at the sister resort, a short walk away.

Marival Distinct — activities

Puerto Vallarta Proper

Puerto Vallarta has the real downtown malecón — a paved seaside promenade, great for daily walks. Resorts here are 5–15 minutes from the airport.

Secrets Vallarta Bay, Puerto Vallarta — $$$$

Pickleball and shuffleboard, adults-only, 10–15 minutes from the malecón.

Secrets Vallarta Bay — activities

Dreams Vallarta Bay Resort & Spa, Puerto Vallarta — $$$

Pickleball, no shuffleboard, family resort, 10–15 minutes from the malecón and the airport.

Dreams Vallarta Bay — rooms

Melia Puerto Vallarta, Marina Vallarta — $

Pickleball (8 courts) and shuffleboard, 5 minutes from the airport, 10 minutes from the malecón. Note: a local club uses the courts on weekday mornings, so afternoons are the safe bet.

Melia Puerto Vallarta

Hyatt Ziva Puerto Vallarta — $$$

3 outdoor pickleball courts, 5 minutes from the malecón (closest of any resort on the list), private cove beach. No shuffleboard.

Hyatt Ziva Puerto Vallarta on Expedia

The Westin Playa Vallarta, Marina Vallarta — $$$

Pickleball and padel courts, newest all-inclusive of the bunch (opened May 2026), minutes from the airport and downtown. No shuffleboard.

The Westin Playa Vallarta

Velas Vallarta Suite Resort, Marina Vallarta — $$

2 pickleball courts, all-suite, about 10 minutes from the malecón. No shuffleboard, and reviewers mention timeshare sales pressure.

Velas Vallarta on Air Canada Vacations

Left off the list: Villa del Palmar in Puerto Vallarta — cheapest, but it's mid-rebuild with amenities disappearing, so we're skipping it.

My take: Marival Emotions is still the best all-round fit, Melia is the best value in town — and the kindest to the two solo rooms — and Secrets is now a real contender, since it's just the six adults: adults-only, pickleball and shuffleboard, close to the malecón.
