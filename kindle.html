Langley · A5

Oak tree concerns lead Langley Township council to review tree bylaw

Township council passed a motion asking staff to work with resident Bob Langston, who wants to remove an oak above his water line and plant a comparable tree elsewhere — he fears its growth could damage the line. Under the current bylaw he cannot legally remove it while the line is intact, so the bylaw effectively requires homeowners to wait for damage before acting. Coun. Blair Whitmarsh moved the motion; it passed 7–2. The Township is now expected to review its overall tree bylaw, adopted in 2019. Fines for cutting without a permit can be steep — a resident recently faced $49,000 in fines for felling 11 trees.
