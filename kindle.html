Canada · Grocery bills · A2

Loblaw warns food prices will rise in 2027 as suppliers demand increases above inflation

Loblaw told investors this week that the big food companies supplying its shelves are demanding price increases above the rate of inflation, and shoppers will feel the squeeze more in 2027 once the holiday price freeze lifts. CEO Per Bank said supplier increases are “over and above the inflation rate right now”; the dollar value of proposed supplier increases jumped 120% in Q4 vs a year ago. Meanwhile Loblaw’s Q3 profit rose to $1.19 billion, up from $794 million a year earlier. Statistics Canada says food bought from stores cost 2% more year over year in October, and grocery prices are still 33% higher than in September 2020.

Canada · Tariffs · A3

Canada sees ‘little movement’ from US on tariff relief as 50% duties loom in January

Dominic LeBlanc, the minister responsible for Canada–US trade, told the Advisory Committee on Canada–US Economic Relations on Thursday that Washington has shown little movement on tariff relief, with duties on Canadian steel, autos and lumber set to rise to 50% on January 1, 2027. Talks broke down in August and, despite weekly contact since, there has been little progress. Ottawa is aiming for an interim deal by the end of the year rather than a full settlement. Langley · Holiday market · A5

A New Fraser Valley Holiday Tradition Begins with the Langley Christmas Village

Langley Christmas Village opens November 26 at 11am, a new European-style holiday market in downtown Langley City at 20340 Fraser Highway (in front of Timms Community Centre), running daily through December 24 with free admission and no ticket required. It features wooden chalets, artisans, live entertainment, a curling rink, festive treats and holiday drinks, with 85+ vendors. Langley City’s annual Magic of Christmas and tree-lighting ceremony takes place there Friday, November 27, 5–10pm, with the tree lighting at 6pm. Presented by Parfait Productions Inc., the team behind North Vancouver’s Shipyards Christmas Market.
