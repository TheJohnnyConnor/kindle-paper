Langley · Targeted killing suspected · A7

IHIT identifies victim found in Langley vehicle fire as Tushar Sharma, 25, of Surrey, says evidence suggests targeted incident

The Integrated Homicide Investigation Team has identified the victim found in a Langley vehicle fire as Tushar Sharma, 25, of Surrey. Langley RCMP responded at about 10:26 p.m. on Oct 29 to a vehicle fire in the 23300-block of Rawlinson Crescent; firefighters found what appeared to be human remains while extinguishing the blaze. IHIT released Sharma’s name and photo on Nov 17 to further its homicide investigation; investigators say the evidence gathered so far suggests the incident was targeted, though no motive has been established and no suspects publicly identified. Sharma had no criminal record and limited known contacts in Canada. Anyone with information is asked to contact IHIT at 1-877-551-IHIT (4448) or ihitinfo@rcmp-grc.gc.ca (IHIT file 2026-1052).
