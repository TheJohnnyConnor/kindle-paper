Canada · Sanctions return · A5

Minister Anand announces reimposition of sanctions against Iranian entities

Foreign Affairs Minister Anita Anand announced Nov 10 that Canada is reimposing sanctions against 11 Iranian entities under the Special Economic Measures (Iran) Regulations, for contributing to the regime’s proliferation-sensitive nuclear activities and ballistic missile program or financing its malign activities. Announced at the G7 Foreign Ministers’ Meeting in Paris; the entities include Iran’s Nuclear Fuel Production and Procurement Company, the Nuclear Reactors Fuel Company, the National Iranian Oil Company, the National Iranian Tanker Company, Kala Naft, Naftiran Intertrade, Bank Mellat, Bank Melli, the Iran Bank of Industry and Mine, the Export Development Bank of Iran, and the Iran Insurance Company. These entities were previously delisted in 2016 under the JCPOA; UN sanctions were reinstated in September 2025.
