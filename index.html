Langley · BC time change · A6

Potential Impacts Following BC's Move to Pacific Time (PCT)

Starting Sunday, November 1, 2026, British Columbia will begin observing Pacific Time (PCT) year-round, meaning clocks will no longer fall back. The Township of Langley says it has been preparing its technology systems and services for the change and working with software vendors on updates, but some systems or applications may still be affected during the transition. Residents may notice time discrepancies when adding Township events or registered programs to personal calendars, depending on calendar provider, app settings or device configuration; the Township advises checking the PDF registration confirmation received at registration to verify a program's scheduled time, and manually updating or removing and re-adding a calendar entry after November 1 if needed. Program schedules will not change — for example, a Sunday class starting at 10am will continue to start at 10am on November 1 and thereafter. Residents should also confirm device and calendar settings are using Pacific Time (PCT), and report any Township service displaying an incorrect time via tol.ca/help.
