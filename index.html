Foreign homebuyers ban extension proposed to Jan 1, 2029

Budget 2026, tabled Nov 5, proposes extending the ban on foreign purchases of existing residential property to Jan 1, 2029, while opening newly built homes to foreign buyers. The ban has been in force since Jan 1, 2023. Americans with Canadian ancestry may be exempt: since Bill C-3 took effect Dec 15, 2025, far more Americans count as Canadian citizens through ancestry, though they need a citizenship certificate (processing about 34 months as of Oct 2026). The extension is still at the proposal stage.
