Canada · Byelections · A6

Prime Minister Mark Carney calls six federal byelections for December 7

Voters in six federal ridings in Quebec, Ontario, and Saskatchewan will head to the polls December 7, after Prime Minister Mark Carney called the byelections Sunday. The Quebec ridings are Laurier—Sainte-Marie, Rosemont—La Petite-Patrie, and Saint-Hyacinthe—Bagot—Acton (seats previously held by the Liberals, the NDP, and the Bloc Québécois respectively); the others are Brantford—Brant South and Scarborough North in Ontario, and Yorkton—Melville in Saskatchewan. The byelections follow MPs quitting the House for diplomatic appointments, provincial politics, or retirement — Scarborough North’s former Liberal Shaun Chen vacated in August citing ongoing medical issues related to a vehicle crash in 2025. Carney’s Liberals currently hold a majority after winning three byelections earlier this year.
