Langley · Township Council · A5

Township Council Orders Independent Forensic Audit of Township Finances

At its first business meeting, the newly sworn-in Township Council voted to order an independent forensic audit of the Township’s finances, borrowing, capital projects, land transactions, agreements and contracts, covering November 7, 2022 to the present. The audit team reports directly to Council and includes, at minimum, a chartered professional accountant with forensic audit experience plus an experienced investigator.

Scope includes the finances, borrowing, reserves and internal borrowing of the Township and the Township of Langley Housing Trust, through which approximately $177 million was borrowed. Mayor Jay Lundgren: “We said we would open the books in our first 100 days. It’s been less than 48 hours since we were sworn in, and we’re throwing the books open.” A Standing Audit Committee will meet at least monthly in public, and staff must report within 45 days on the status and cost of every capital project under way or approved.
