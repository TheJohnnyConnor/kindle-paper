Flooding rains prompt evacuations, shelter-in-place alerts in British Columbia

Canada · Trade war · A3

Canada to sue Stellantis as Trump trade war reshapes production plans

Industry Minister Mélanie Joly said Thursday the government served Stellantis with a formal notice of default, giving the company 30 days to address concerns or face legal action, after Stellantis announced plans to sell its idled Brampton, Ontario assembly plant to armoured-vehicle maker Roshel and shift Jeep Compass production to Illinois amid U.S. tariffs. PM Mark Carney called the move “unacceptable” and a “betrayal” of workers. Stellantis Canada CEO Trevor Longley reportedly said Thursday the company would return $24 million in federal subsidies.
