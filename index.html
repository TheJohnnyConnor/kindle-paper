Canada · Immigration targets · A3

Canada Sets 2027–29 Immigration Targets as Budget Adds 200,000 One‑Time Pathway

In mid-November the government published IRCC’s 2027–2029 levels plan, fixing annual permanent resident admissions at 380,000 and setting a midpoint target of 370,000 new temporary residents (220,000 workers and 150,000 students). Budget 2026 creates a separate, time-limited program of 200,000 permanent-residence spots for people already in Canada who qualify by the end of 2027. The plan makes the Rural Community Immigration Pilot permanent from January 1, 2027, with a 5,500 PR target for 2027. Statistics Canada data and analysts warn that large existing temporary populations — about 1.5 million work-permit holders, roughly 399,000 study permits, and roughly 530,000 asylum-related claimants and protected persons — plus backlog and processing delays increase the risk that actual flows will exceed headline limits.
