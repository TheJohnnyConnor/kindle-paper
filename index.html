Canada · Tropical forests · A3

Prime Minister Carney announces Canada’s landmark contribution to protect the world’s tropical forests

Prime Minister Mark Carney, meeting Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva on the margins of the ASEAN Summit in Manila, announced an initial contribution of up to $560 million over four years to the Tropical Forest Forever Facility (TFFF), co-led by Brazil and Norway. The mechanism mobilises public and private investment to protect tropical forests, preserve biodiversity, and support the communities that depend on them, aiming to protect more than one billion hectares across more than 70 countries annually. It builds on Canada’s $3.8 billion A Force of Nature strategy, a further $1.2 billion for oceans, and nearly $6 billion in international climate finance committed in the Spring Economic Update 2026, bringing Canada’s climate-finance goal to over $13 billion by 2031.

Canada · U.S. trade · A4

Carney Says Canada Will Sign U.S. Trade Deal Only if Its Auto Sector Can Compete

Prime Minister Mark Carney said in Manila on Saturday that Canada will only sign a trade agreement with the United States if it allows Canadian automakers and parts suppliers to remain competitive. He said talks with President Donald Trump are making progress after a lengthy conversation, though an agreement remained out of reach as of Saturday’s briefing. The auto file is one of the hardest issues in negotiations that stalled in August. The stakes are highest in Ontario, where auto manufacturing employs more than 95,000 people and exported $60 billion in vehicles and parts to the U.S. in 2025 — 96 per cent of the province’s automotive exports.
