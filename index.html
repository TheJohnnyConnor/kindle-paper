Canada · Trade deal · A6

Canada signs trade deal with UAE in push for non-US markets

Canada and the United Arab Emirates have signed a comprehensive trade deal to lower barriers between the economies and boost investment. Prime Minister Mark Carney and Abu Dhabi Crown Prince Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan presided over the signing of the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA), which aims to cut tariffs, reduce red tape and improve market access for businesses in both countries. Carney has pledged to double Ottawa’s non-US trade over the next decade as US President Donald Trump’s tariffs have rippled through Canada’s economy. CEPA is expected to enter into force in 2027.

Langley · Land dispute · A7

Metro Vancouver appeal fails in multi-million-dollar land dispute in Langley

Metro Vancouver’s attempt to trim $450,000 off the price it paid for land to build an expansion to a sewage treatment plant in Langley was denied by the B.C. Court of Appeal. The case was about the proper price to expropriate land from S&R Sawmills, a 15-acre lot on the shore of the Fraser River just west of the Golden Ears Bridge, needed to expand the Northwest Langley Wastewater Treatment Plant, which serves about 30,000 people in Langley. Metro Vancouver paid $37.65 million in compensation to S&R, both for the actual land and for the value of a lease. The original December 10, 2025 ruling found Metro had underpaid and ordered an additional $1.89 million; the appeal was dismissed, so Metro must pay the full amount.
