Canada · Real-time payments · A2

Canada launches the Real-Time Rail, bringing 24/7 payment infrastructure to the national economy

Payments Canada today announced the launch of Canada’s Real-Time Rail (RTR), a new national payment system enabling instant speed and settlement, real-time cash-flow visibility and data-rich messaging through existing banking and payment channels, 24 hours a day, every day of the year. The launch marks a major milestone in modernizing the infrastructure underpinning Canada’s economy. The C.D. Howe Institute projects the RTR will generate up to $14.5 billion in cost savings over the next decade, and Canada is the first G7 country to deploy a real-time payment system with mandatory centralized fraud services at launch. Built with CGI, Deloitte Canada, IBM Canada and Interac Corp., using ISO 20022 messaging.

British Columbia · 9-1-1 goes digital · A6

BC RCMP completes transition to Next Generation 9-1-1

The BC RCMP has successfully completed its transition to the digital emergency network, Next Generation 9-1-1 (NG9-1-1), after police dispatchers and call takers from the final two Operational Communications Centres — in Coquitlam and Langley Detachments — were migrated on November 3rd. The federally mandated transition from analog to the digital network is being led by E-Comm 9-1-1 for the province. There will be no noticeable difference in how the public accesses help during this initial phase; the only immediate change is a new E-Comm technology solution to quickly manage abandoned 9-1-1 calls. No deadline is set yet for the additional capabilities (such as real-time text) in later phases.
