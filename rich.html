Canada · Jobs · A5

Unemployment hits 6.7% in October

Statistics Canada said Friday that Canada’s unemployment rate rose to 6.7 per cent in October, up from 6.5 per cent in September — a six-month high and the second straight monthly increase. The economy shed a net 4,700 jobs in October, after losses of 42,000 in August and 68,000 in September. Youth unemployment jumped to 13.7 per cent, up from 13.0 per cent. The employment rate slipped to 60.5 per cent. Wholesale and retail trade led losses, shedding 29,000 jobs. Average hourly wages rose 2.2 per cent year over year.

BC · Search dogs · A7

Avalanche search dog associations warn of public safety risk after Parks Canada cuts Banff K9 program

The Canadian Avalanche Rescue Dog Association and the British Columbia Search Dog Association have asked Parks Canada to “immediately reverse” the cancellation of its Banff K9 program, warning it raises risks to rescuers and the public. Parks Canada is eliminating its sole remaining dog handler position at Banff National Park as part of about C$145 million in budget cuts, ending a program that ran for 55 years. The last dog in the program, German Shepherd Leroy, retired in August 2026 after seven years on the job, in which he helped track 156 people, found 57 who were lost in the wilderness, and helped solve two murders in Banff. A petition calling for the role to be restored has drawn more than 5,000 signatures.
