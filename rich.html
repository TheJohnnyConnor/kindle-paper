Canada · EV & trade · A1

Prime Minister Carney advances Canada’s strategic partnership with China across trade, commerce, and culture

Prime Minister Mark Carney met Chinese President Xi Jinping in Shenzhen on Tuesday, November 17, ahead of the APEC summit, extending and expanding the preliminary Canada–China trade arrangement. China will cut tariffs on about C$400 million in Canadian exports — C$116 million in frozen pork cuts and C$285 million in fish and seafood — and suspend anti-discrimination tariffs from January 1, 2027 to December 31, 2028. In exchange, Canada extends steel and aluminum remission measures by two years and adds 32 tariff items. Carney said Canada is well ahead of schedule on boosting exports to China by 50% by the end of the decade. Chinese automakers BYD, Geely and Chery have taken steps to enter the Canadian market since Ottawa approved annual imports of 49,000 cars at a 6.1% tariff rate.
